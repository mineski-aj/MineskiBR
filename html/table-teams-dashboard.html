Arrange the squads shown on the Ingame LED scene (/html/ledoverlay.html): 5 on the left panel, 5 on the right, and the 2 featured squads on the bottom strip (left / right). Each card shows the squad's current Grand Finals rank, elimination points and total points — this tab only decides where a squad sits. A squad can only hold one panel slot (a featured squad may also be on a panel). Changes save automatically and reach the overlay within a few seconds. Until you arrange it, the LED follows the standings order.
